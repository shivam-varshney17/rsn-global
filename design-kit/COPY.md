## Buying questions

**Can I browse without joining?** Yes. Global Select is open to browse. Membership is required to order.

**How do I order?** Explore a product, check its details, then use your member account to order. Read how membership works.

**Where do you deliver?** Check the current delivery information for your country and location before ordering. View delivery information.

**Which currency will I pay in?** You are browsing the selected country/currency view. Check the confirmed price and currency in the product and checkout information.

**How do returns work?** Review the applicable conditions for the product and market before buying. Read the returns information.

The generic policy answers link to approved existing information. Replace them with more specific verified answers when available; don't invent delivery coverage, duties or return windows to fill the space.
