A visitor should understand within the first screen: what they can discover, why this is a considered selection, browsing is open, membership is required to order and where to explore the collection. The tone is a knowledgeable, welcoming shopkeeper. Keep clear shopping information beneath the atmosphere.

No festive design, festival campaign, seasonal decoration or countdown. No B2B procurement form, wholesale-institution positioning, supplier-first hero, shipping-box hero or demo operating metrics. Keep membership fees off the homepage. Do not invent free/paid/lifetime/invitation/limited terms, discounts, benefits, endorsements, availability, origin, returns, delivery promises or product performance claims.

## Art direction

Use warm cream #F1E9DA, espresso #1F1712 and restrained brass #C9A25A. Let real merchandise supply colour. Use the actual existing RSN ONE mark. Fraunces for expressive readable editorial headings; Inter for navigation, body, product details and buttons. Retain the project's existing font integration instead of loading duplicates. Use sentence case, short readable labels and careful alignment.

The recommended first screen is a split hero: clear copy and genuinely available products shown beautifully. Make breadth visible only where real assortment supports it. A beauty-only hero makes the offer read as beauty-led. Use believable materials, consistent light and readable packaging. Real people, sourcing and service images must represent genuine work. Use the media prompts with actual product reference assets; do not invent labelled products or create false operational evidence.

Reject repetitive decorative cards, oversized pills, floating badges, artificial scarcity, endless abstract headlines and animation-dependent content. Use intentional contrast between product imagery, orderly grids and one warm membership section. Aim for desire plus comprehension, rather than visual noise.

## Required homepage

1. Compact header: RSN ONE, Global Select, Shop by category, How membership works, Search, Account and Bag. Expose the supported country/currency state without an opening selection wall. Supplier/admin routes belong in secondary navigation/footer.
2. Product-led hero. Proposed headline: Global finds. / For your everyday. Explain the offer plainly. Primary: Explore Global Select. Secondary: How it works. Clarification: Browse freely. Membership is required to order.
3. Categories within the first scroll. Use familiar labels and show only categories with a useful actual assortment. Proposed directions: Beauty & Care, Health & Wellness, Home & Living, Gifts.
4. Four to eight actual featured products. Real brand, short name, verified origin if relevant, exact authorised price state and a product-detail action. No fabricated bestseller or savings labels.
5. Concise membership explanation after the products. Browse first. / Join when it fits. Explain open browsing, the actual membership information and member ordering. No invented fees/benefits.
6. One evidence-led selection story with an authentic image and caption. Establish who selects, an actual reason for inclusion, inspectable product information and the genuine support process. Use documented answers instead of claiming tests or inspections that did not occur.
7. Practical questions: browsing, ordering, delivery, currency and returns. Use existing approved country-specific policies.
8. Final invitation and useful footer. Browse Global Select remains primary. Newsletter/concierge use the real working integrations and remain secondary.
