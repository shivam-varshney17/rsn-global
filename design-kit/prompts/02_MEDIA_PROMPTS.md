# Product-image and film briefs

Use these with actual approved product reference photographs. The references are prerequisites to a final product campaign, not optional inspiration. Generated scenes may compose the supplied products, but must preserve the actual packaging, proportions, labels, colours and identity. If the model cannot reproduce those accurately, composite the original product cut-outs into the scene instead.

The existing preview uses original unbranded illustrations only. It makes no claim that those objects are sold. The prompts below do not license reference-brand imagery or create genuine customer/service evidence.

## 1 / Homepage still life — Editorial direction

**Input:** three to five genuinely available products spanning the actual assortment; approved packaging photographs and the RSN ONE palette. Do not add a category the catalogue does not support.

**Prompt:** Create an editorial retail still life for RSN ONE Global Select using the uploaded product references exactly. Compose a carefully balanced selection on a warm cream surface with a natural linen detail, a restrained dark wood plane and soft directional morning light. Make each product recognisable at a realistic relative scale. Allow visual breathing room; place the principal grouping around the centre-right with space between packaging silhouettes. The feeling is welcoming, considered everyday retail for households in Nepal and India. Palette comes from the real merchandise, warm cream #F1E9DA and espresso #1F1712. No festive decor, extra brand labels, invented packaging, gold luxury effects, decorative typography or added text. Preserve all source-product details. Produce a clean 4:3 landscape composition at 1600×1200 or larger.

**Mobile companion:** Make an independent 4:5 composition from the same exact products and lighting. Keep every important package within the central 70% so a small-screen crop remains informative. No copy overlay required; the website places text separately.

## 2 / The useful collection — Storefront direction

**Prompt:** Using the exact approved product references, create a restrained wide retail composition on a warm pale background. Give the assortment clarity: readable labels, clean silhouettes, truthful relative size and consistent soft shadows. Organise by use rather than a rigid ceremonial arrangement. Keep the frame compact enough to sit beside a short shopping proposition. No invented objects, added logos, promotions or seasonal styling. Output landscape 16:9 plus a deliberate mobile crop. Preserve packaging without generative rewriting; use original product composites where needed.

## 3 / The everyday journal — lifestyle direction

**Prompt:** Build a believable everyday household scene around the supplied actual products. Use an ordinary, well-composed lived-in surface: a shelf, dressing area or small table with natural materials and honest textures. Keep the products the point of the picture. The setting should feel relevant to contemporary households in Nepal and India without relying on religious symbols, exotic stereotypes or expensive hotel styling. Warm natural light, visible practical use and gentle tonal depth. Preserve all product identities. No invented user endorsement, extra goods or festive elements. Produce a wide image with a calm left area for the cream reading panel, plus a separate mobile composition with the products grouped clearly.

## 4 / Category images

Produce four consistent compositions only for actual available categories. Use 1:1 images at 900×900 or larger. Keep the lighting, surface height and background treatment consistent. Each image should communicate its category at thumbnail size. The category name is rendered by the website, not generated into the image.

- Beauty & Care: actual skincare/personal-care goods, readable packaging and believable scale.
- Health & Wellness: actual eligible goods; no invented therapeutic effect, clinician imagery or certification.
- Home & Living: actual home/everyday objects shown in a useful context.
- Gifts: genuinely suitable catalogue goods with restrained reusable presentation; no seasonal campaign.

## 5 / Product-detail packshots

Preserve the original product and packaging. Use a uniform warm neutral background, consistent camera height, a soft grounding shadow and accurate colour. Deliver a front view, informative alternate view and a scale/use view where the product permits. Do not alter net weight, ingredients, seals, expiry markings or claims. Use retouching of real assets rather than synthetic replacement of information customers need to inspect.

## 6 / Selection and service story

**Shoot brief, not evidence to fabricate:** photograph the real selector reviewing a real candidate, or actual packing/support work for the collection. Capture an identifiable product detail and a captionable action. Record who, where, what is happening and why it matters. If authentic material is unavailable, use a product editorial image and conservative copy; do not generate people/facilities and call them RSN's operation.

## 7 / Optional short homepage film

**Input:** exact approved products and a final static hero.

**Film brief:** 8–12 seconds, three calm shots: natural light across the actual packaging; a hand reaching for one genuine everyday object; the selection together in its household setting. Use real product close-ups and a believable action. Keep actual scale and labels intact. No unearned testimonial, medical transformation, shipping promise, factory ownership cue or invented product functionality. Let the scene finish naturally. Supply a high-quality static poster, muted/no audio default, compact web encodes and a separate mobile cut. Respect reduced motion and keep the image/text experience complete without the film.

## Asset delivery checklist

For every asset record the actual product IDs, rights/approval, reference inputs, final filename, dimensions, intended crop, alt text and whether it is a photograph, composite or illustration. Aim for efficient WebP/AVIF web derivatives after visual review. Keep the original high-resolution master separately. Do not let a large film delay the first useful image or shopping action.
