Design a new homepage for RSN ONE, centred on Global Select.

BUSINESS AND POSITIONING

RSN ONE serves households in Nepal and India. Its products are sourced globally.

Global Select is the main shopping collection: selected beauty, wellbeing, personal care, home and everyday products from international brands.

RSN ONE is the curator and the membership relationship behind that collection.

Visitors should quickly understand:
1. What products they can discover.
2. Why RSN ONE selected them.
3. That browsing is open.
4. That membership is required to order.
5. How to explore the collection.

Make the first impression welcoming, credible and desirable. The products should feel relevant to everyday life in Nepal and India.

Keep membership fees off the homepage. Do not describe membership as free, paid, lifetime, invitation-only or limited until those terms are confirmed.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Create a warm editorial retail experience with clear shopping navigation.

The mood should be:
- Thoughtful and confident.
- Human and grounded.
- Premium through photography, typography and careful selection.
- Comfortable enough for everyday shopping.
- Clear enough for someone encountering RSN ONE for the first time.

Use the existing brand identity as the foundation. Give the homepage a stronger balance between atmosphere and practical shopping information.

Reference directions:
- Aesop: precise product categories and considered brand communication.
- TOAST: everyday-life storytelling, materials and a human editorial voice.
- MUJI: understandable categories and useful products presented plainly.

Translate those principles into RSN ONE’s own identity. Additional Pinterest and Behance research should favour complete retail websites and mobile layouts, with specific references cited after inspection.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Colour:
- Warm cream for the main reading and browsing surfaces.
- Espresso brown for navigation accents, selected brand sections and membership.
- Restrained brass for small highlights, rules and details.
- Natural colours from the merchandise and photography.

Existing brand colours include:
Cream: #F1E9DA
Espresso: #1F1712
Brass: #C9A25A

Let product imagery provide visual variety. Keep the interface palette disciplined.

Typography:
- An expressive, readable editorial serif for the main headline and selected section headings.
- A clean sans serif for navigation, product information, descriptions and buttons.
- Fraunces and Inter are already available in the project.
- Use sentence case for most headings.
- Keep uppercase labels short and readable.
- Reserve condensed campaign typography for occasional campaigns.

Layout:
- A clear grid with deliberate alignment.
- Generous spacing around important content.
- Compact enough that visitors reach products quickly.
- Simple buttons and restrained borders.
- A mix of editorial image compositions and orderly product grids.

The visual character should come from real merchandise, strong composition and typography. Avoid repeating decorative cards, oversized pills, floating badges, artificial urgency or elaborate animation across every section.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

1. COMPACT HEADER

Include:
RSN ONE logo
Global Select
Shop by category
How membership works
Search
Account
Bag

Make Global Select the obvious shopping destination.

Use ordinary labels people understand. Supplier recruitment and administrative access belong in secondary navigation or the footer.

Provide a visible country/currency control when supported. Nepal and India should feel explicitly considered, without forcing an opening country-selection screen.

2. CLEAR, PRODUCT-LED HERO

Preferred desktop composition:
A split layout with a readable text area and strong product photography.

Suggested copy:

Eyebrow:
RSN ONE · GLOBAL SELECT

Headline:
Global finds.
For your everyday.

Supporting text:
Beauty, wellbeing and everyday goods, thoughtfully selected from Korea, Thailand and beyond. Discover your next favourite, closer to home.

Primary action:
Explore Global Select

Secondary action:
How it works

Small clarification:
Browse freely. Join the club when you’re ready to order.

The headline may be refined, but the supporting sentence must plainly explain the offer.

Use real products from the available collection. Show recognisable packaging, materials and useful context.

A beauty-only hero will make the business read as beauty-led. If the intended offer is broader, show a carefully composed selection from several genuinely stocked categories.

Keep the main action visible and readable on mobile. An optional film must support a strong static image and must not delay understanding or interaction.

3. SHOP BY CATEGORY

Bring categories into the first scroll.

Use familiar, merchandise-led labels such as:
Beauty & Care
Health & Wellness
Home & Living
Gifts

Only feature categories with a useful available assortment.

Image-led category tiles should show what each category contains. Country and brand are secondary discovery routes.

4. FEATURED GLOBAL SELECT PRODUCTS

Show four to eight carefully chosen products.

Suggested heading:
Meet the selection.

Each product should communicate:
- A strong product photograph.
- Brand.
- Short, readable product name.
- Verified origin where relevant.
- Correct price and membership state.
- A clear product-detail action.

Keep long technical descriptions on product pages.

Use actual products and genuine availability. Maintain the existing backend rules for prices, member access and purchasing.

5. MEMBERSHIP, EXPLAINED SIMPLY

Introduce a concise membership section after the first product selection.

Suggested heading:
Browse first.
Join when it fits.

Explain:
Explore freely.
Understand membership.
Order as a member.

Use short, concrete sentences and a link to membership information.

Explain real benefits clearly. Keep fees and unconfirmed founding offers off the homepage.

6. WHY THESE PRODUCTS

Give visitors evidence of the selection process.

Use an authentic sourcing, inspection, packing or dispatch photograph with a useful caption.

Explain:
Who selects the products?
What makes a product worth including?
What information can shoppers inspect?
How does RSN ONE support the purchase?

Connect the brand story to a concrete customer benefit.

Keep one strong sourcing section instead of several sections repeating abstract ideas about care, journeys and discovery.

7. PRACTICAL BUYING QUESTIONS

Answer a short set of questions:
Can I browse without joining?
How do I order?
Where do you deliver?
Which currency will I pay in?
How do returns work?

Keep the language direct.

Delivery coverage, timelines, duties and return conditions must reflect actual operations in each market. Targeting Nepal and India does not automatically establish identical service in both countries.

8. FINAL INVITATION AND FOOTER

Suggested closing:
Find something for your everyday.

Primary action:
Browse Global Select

Secondary action:
How membership works

Footer:
Customer support
Delivery
Returns
Membership
About RSN ONE
Sourcing
Supplier information
Newsletter
Legal information

Keep newsletter signup and concierge access available without competing with the first shopping action.

PHOTOGRAPHY

Prioritise:
- Actual products currently available.
- Clear packaging and realistic scale.
- Consistent lighting.
- Natural materials and believable environments.
- People using products where suitable.
- Genuine sourcing and service photography.

Distinguish international brands from RSN ONE’s own products. Do not visually imply that RSN ONE manufactures every item.

Use product photographs that make the selection easier to understand. Decorative lifestyle imagery should have a clear role.

COPY STYLE

Write as a knowledgeable, welcoming shopkeeper.

Use concrete words: product, brand, origin, selection, browse, membership, delivery.

Keep sentences short and specific.

A little poetry can create atmosphere, but each major section must communicate useful information.

Reduce repeated abstract headlines such as:
“Some things find you.”
“A world, considered.”
“Chosen slowly.”

Give the visitor something new to understand in each section.

MOBILE EXPERIENCE

Design mobile as a primary experience.

Ensure:
- The product proposition and primary action appear early.
- Text remains readable without zooming.
- Navigation labels are understandable.
- Product cards work in a two-column grid.
- Touch targets are comfortable.
- Consent UI does not cover the main action.
- The concierge stays unobtrusive.
- Essential content remains visible without animation.
- Images load efficiently and layouts remain stable.

DESIGN SUCCESS CRITERIA

After seeing the first screen briefly, a new visitor should be able to say:

“RSN ONE offers selected global products for everyday life. I can browse Global Select, and membership is how I order.”

The page should help them find a relevant product, understand the club and trust the next step.

Produce:
1. Desktop and mobile homepage designs.
2. A clear typography, colour and spacing system.
3. Final proposed copy for every section.
4. A rationale connecting design choices to the audience and positioning.
5. Specific reference links, distinguishing inspected examples from suggestions.

For implementation, preserve the existing authentication, catalogue, membership and pricing logic.
